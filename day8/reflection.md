# Course Reflection

The most difficult part of JavaScript Fundamentals for me was understanding how the different concepts fit together when writing code. Some ideas did not make sense immediately, and that could feel frustrating. I worked through the difficulty by trying things out and returning to the parts I did not understand. I am still learning, but I have realized that I do not need to master everything at once. I can work on it in bits until it becomes clearer.

Based on the feedback I received, I would improve the design of my capstone. I would take more time to think about how the pieces fit together and how someone will experience the project before I focus on the details. I would also use feedback earlier, while there is still time to make thoughtful changes. Improving the design would help make the capstone clearer, more useful, and easier to use.

Next, I want to learn more about systems design. I love thinking about how different parts of an application work together, and the topic gives me goosebumps in the best way. I want to understand how to design systems that can handle real users, growing traffic, and unexpected problems. I know there is a lot to learn, so I will keep taking it one concept at a time, asking questions, and practicing by designing systems of my own.
